# Spacing and Retention Planning

## Inputs
Retention deadline, consequence of forgetting, current accuracy, item difficulty, prior familiarity, available study opportunities, and review time.

## Initial schedule
If no history exists, propose a modest starting heuristic: first retrieval shortly after encoding; another later the same day or next day; further retrieval after several days; later retrieval after one or more weeks; final check near the required date. Adapt to deadline and availability. These are examples, not optimal universal intervals.

## Adaptive rules
- Correct and easy → lengthen next interval.
- Correct but effortful → repeat sooner.
- Partial → correct missing detail and shorten interval.
- Incorrect → feedback, cue correction, retry after short delay.
- Repeated confusion → redesign cue, do not merely increase repetition.
- High consequence → independent verification and final check.
