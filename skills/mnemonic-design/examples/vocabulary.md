# Example: Vocabulary — Keyword Method

**Target:** English *bark*, meaning the outer covering of a tree.

**Technique:** keyword association + image.<br>
**Cue:** imagine a large tree wearing a dog-bark patterned coat.<br>
**Mapping:** the tree's outer coat represents tree bark; the dog sound is only a sound hook.<br>
**Discriminator:** tree bark is not a dog's bark.

**Recall:** "What is the word for the outer covering of a tree?"<br>
**Answer:** bark.<br>
**Status:** Designed, untested. Attempt recall before marking tested.
